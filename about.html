Our Story

From Newtown to all of Kolkata

Sky-High Properties was founded with one clear goal — to bring trust, transparency, and expertise to real estate transactions in Kolkata. Starting from our base in Newtown, Kolkata, we have grown into one of the region's most reliable property consultancies.

With over 10 years of combined experience, our team has guided hundreds of clients through the journey of buying, selling, and investing in property — always with honesty and a deep understanding of the local market.

  • Local Market Expertise — In-depth knowledge of Kolkata, New Town, and Rajarhat property markets.
  • Verified Listings — Every property we list is personally inspected and verified by our team.
